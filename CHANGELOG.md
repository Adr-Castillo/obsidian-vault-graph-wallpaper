# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Shows the live Obsidian graph view as the X11 desktop wallpaper on Linux.
- Interactive mode: zoom, drag, click a node to open it, and cursor gravity that
  bends nearby nodes as the pointer passes.
- Backgrounds: theme colour, a built-in space scene (stars over a lunar horizon),
  or your own image.
- Launch Obsidian at login (minimised), and keep a snapshot as the system
  wallpaper so the graph shows before Obsidian has loaded.
- Does no work while idle; repaints only when something actually moves.
